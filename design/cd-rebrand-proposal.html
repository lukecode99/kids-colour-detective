A proposal, not a build — per the standing rule, no rebuild code gets written until you've signed a design off. The only invented thing on the page is the example sampled colour, #7F9B6E.

The one real decision

Everything above is mechanical. This isn't: the site is light and the app is dark, and the app is dark for a reason — it wraps a live camera preview and a colour swatch, and a bright surround shifts how you perceive the colour sitting next to it. So there are two honest ways to "match the site", and they trade brand fidelity against the thing the app is actually for. Both are below with identical content.

Notes

Both skins drop the blue→purple gradient on the active tab: the site has a single accent and no gradient anywhere, so keeping it is the one thing that would still read as a different product. The green/amber calibration states in the camera screen map onto the site's existing --good and --promo pairs rather than needing new colours. Roughly three quarters of the app's colour usage already goes through theme.ts (145 references against 50 hardcoded values, 26 of which are plain black or white), so whichever you pick, most of the work is one file.
